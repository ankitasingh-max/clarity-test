3. What to check in Clarity

  • Dashboard: Live users should show 1 while you are on this page.
  • Recordings: usually 5 to 10 minutes after you finish, once the session has ended (30 minutes of inactivity).
  • Heatmaps: about 30 minutes after the first data.
  • Filters: filter by the custom tags study, test and step to find your session.
  • Masking: the text you typed should be hidden in the recording.
  • Dashboard date range: set it to include today.
